## `at-neufoe2.pdf`

Austrian Federal Ministry of Finance form “NeuFoe 2 - Erklärung gemäß § 4 bzw.
§ 5a iVm § 4 Neugründungs-Förderungsgesetz” (version 1.6).

- Source: <https://service.bmf.gv.at/service/anwend/formulare/show_det.asp?Typ=SD&STyp=&MIdVal=1124&s=NeuFoe2>
- Direct PDF: <https://formulare.bmf.gv.at/service/formulare/inter-Steuern/pdfs/9999/NeuFoe2.pdf>

This is an official Austrian government work. Under §7 of the Austrian
copyright law (Urheberrechtsgesetz), acts, ordinances, and official works issued
by public bodies generally carry no copyright protection. The document is used
here as a redistribution-safe real-world AcroForm test fixture.
